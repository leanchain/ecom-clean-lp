# AI citation-gap investigation

Use this skill when monitored AI buying answers omit a product, cite competitors, or repeat inaccurate merchant facts.

## Required inputs

- Fixed prompt set
- Named model or answer surface
- Target products and markets
- Timestamped observed answers
- Relevant competitors
- Merchant-controlled product sources

## Expected output

- Visibility and citation matrix
- Incorrect or unsupported claims
- Source-pattern observations
- Candidate controlled-object changes
- Retest plan

## Workflow

1. Classify prompts by intent.
2. Capture answers, citations, merchant attribution, and competitor presence.
3. Inspect cited-source patterns and controlled product facts.
4. Compare absent products with selected alternatives.
5. Form a testable content, catalog, or entity hypothesis.
6. Change one controlled object where possible.
7. Retest the fixed prompt set and document differences.

## Checks

- Product appearance
- Fact accuracy
- Merchant attribution
- Citation sources
- Competitor displacement
- Market and timestamp consistency

## Boundaries

<Callout type="boundary" title="Observation, not guarantee">
Results are point-in-time observations. Retests can show answer changes but do not prove durable placement, search ranking, or revenue causation.
</Callout>
