Case files turn useful field investigations into reusable operating knowledge. The Fieldbook will publish real cases only when the evidence, permissions, and anonymization standard are sufficient.

## Required structure

- Initial signal
- Affected object or journey
- Evidence collected
- Hypotheses considered
- Controlled action
- Verification method
- Observed result
- Remaining uncertainty

## Publication rules

Customer, employee, transaction, and commercially sensitive data must be removed or explicitly approved. Modeled impact cannot be presented as observed revenue.

<Callout type="evidence" title="No invented case studies">
Until a field result is verified and publishable, the Fieldbook will provide templates and methods rather than fictional success stories.
</Callout>

## Case-file template

Use the contribution page to submit an anonymized case. Include source evidence, timestamps, the controlled intervention, and the exact verification step.
