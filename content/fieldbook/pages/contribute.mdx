The Fieldbook begins with structured participation rather than an empty open forum. Useful contributions become reviewed documents, resource entries, or anonymized case files.

## What you can submit

- A common ecommerce problem that is not represented
- A correction supported by a primary source
- A reusable agent skill or human checklist
- An operational playbook
- An open-source project, standard, or official reference
- An anonymized field result

## What a strong submission includes

Name the affected page or proposed title, the problem being solved, intended users, evidence or primary references, known boundaries, and why the material adds something not already covered.

## Review criteria

Submissions are reviewed for operational usefulness, evidence quality, attribution, maintenance status, licensing, safety, and unsupported claims.
