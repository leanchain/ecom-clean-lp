Beseam curates the Commerce Fieldbook but does not claim ownership of the wider ecosystem it references.

## Selection criteria

A project or reference should have a clear commerce use case, identifiable maintainer, official source, and visible license or usage boundary. Primary documentation and standards are preferred for requirements and platform behavior.

A resource may be omitted even when it is popular if its maintenance status, licensing, claims, or operational value cannot be established responsibly.

## Attribution

External projects retain their maintainers, governance, documentation, trademarks, and licenses. Inclusion does not imply endorsement by either party.

## Maturity labels

- **Stable:** appropriate for routine operational use.
- **Reference:** authoritative documentation or a specification used to interpret requirements.
- **Emerging:** useful, but the implementation or ecosystem is still changing.
- **Experimental:** worth observing or testing, but not a settled standard.

## Corrections

Corrections should identify the affected page, current statement, supporting primary source, and proposed change. Material corrections update the review date.

## Contributions

Submissions may propose a problem, skill, playbook, project, correction, or anonymized field result. Promotional links and unsupported outcome claims are not accepted.
