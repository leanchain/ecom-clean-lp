Use this playbook when structured-data diagnostics regress after a controlled storefront change.

## Before changing code

Capture the current rendered page, parser output, visible product facts, active variant, market, and previous known-good evidence if available.

## Recovery sequence

1. Enumerate every Product, ProductGroup, and Offer entity.
2. Identify the template, app, or extension emitting each entity.
3. Compare visible product facts with the graph.
4. Remove or consolidate competing emitters.
5. Correct variant and offer relationships.
6. Retest rendered HTML locally or in preview.
7. Deploy through the normal approval path.
8. Recheck the live page and external diagnostic.

<Callout type="boundary" title="Eligibility remains external">
Recovery proves that the controlled implementation is coherent. Search engines still decide eligibility and display.
</Callout>
