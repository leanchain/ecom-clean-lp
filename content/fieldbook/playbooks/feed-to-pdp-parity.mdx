Use this playbook when product data contradictions cross team or system boundaries.

## Roles

- Catalog owner: confirms authoritative product and variant values.
- Storefront owner: confirms rendered content and structured data.
- Feed owner: confirms transformations and export timing.
- Channel owner: confirms diagnostics and reprocessing state.

## Sequence

1. Select a small affected product and variant set.
2. Freeze observation time, market, and currency.
3. Build the field-level comparison.
4. Assign every contradiction to a source or transformation owner.
5. Correct the authoritative object.
6. Regenerate dependent outputs.
7. Verify storefront and channel separately.

## Exit criteria

- Identifiers, price, availability, title, and images agree.
- Ownership is documented for every transformed field.
- Channel diagnostics reflect the corrected export.
- Monitoring or regression tests cover the failure mode.
