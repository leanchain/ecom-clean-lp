Use this playbook when conversion or support signals suggest a purchase failure but the storefront is not fully down.

## Immediate response

Preserve the affected time window, release context, customer-safe evidence, and any browser or market clustering. Avoid changing multiple systems before a reproducible condition is found.

## Sequence

1. Define the affected step and customer impact.
2. Build a minimal browser, device, market, and consent matrix.
3. Reproduce with approved test data.
4. Correlate browser evidence with traces, errors, and releases.
5. Identify a controlled mitigation or rollback.
6. Verify the original failure path.
7. Add monitoring for the previously invisible step.

## Exit criteria

- The observed failure is reproducible or bounded.
- Mitigation ownership and customer impact are documented.
- Verification covers the original affected conditions.
- Follow-up work addresses detection and regression coverage.
