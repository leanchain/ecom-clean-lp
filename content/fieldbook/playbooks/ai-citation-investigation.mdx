Use this playbook to coordinate discovery, content, catalog, and research owners around a fixed answer-query set.

## Preparation

Define the surfaces, markets, products, prompt intents, observation schedule, and data-retention policy. Record answer and citation evidence consistently.

## Sequence

1. Establish a fixed baseline query set.
2. Capture answer text, cited sources, attribution, and competitors.
3. Inspect merchant-controlled facts and cited third-party facts.
4. Classify gaps as technical, catalog, content, entity, or externally controlled selection.
5. Choose one controlled change with claim support.
6. Retest the same prompt set.
7. Record answer differences without overstating causation.

## Exit criteria

- Baseline and retest conditions are documented.
- Controlled changes remain accurate and useful to buyers.
- Findings distinguish citations, mentions, accuracy, and placement.
- No ranking or revenue guarantee is made.
