Partial purchase failures often remain invisible to uptime checks because the storefront still loads and most sessions complete normally.

## What teams usually observe

- Conversion drops without a complete outage.
- Support reports cluster around one browser, device, market, or payment method.
- Add-to-cart, cart updates, or checkout navigation intermittently fail.
- A recent theme, app, tag, or checkout configuration changed.

## Evidence to collect

Capture the affected URL and journey, product, browser, device, market, consent state, time window, console and network output, storefront events, relevant release history, and authorized payment-test context.

## What not to conclude yet

A correlation with a release or app does not prove root cause. Store-wide uptime does not prove that the transaction path works for every state.

## Investigation workflow

1. Build the smallest useful reproduction matrix.
2. Reproduce the exact step where observed behavior diverges.
3. Inspect page state, network calls, console errors, and button accessibility.
4. Compare affected and unaffected conditions.
5. Narrow the owning component, app, service, or configuration.
6. Apply a controlled fix and rerun the same path.

<Callout type="warning" title="Authorized testing only">
Use approved test products, payment modes, and environments. Do not create destructive production orders or handle sensitive payment data outside authorized systems.
</Callout>

## Verification checklist

- The original failure conditions were retested.
- The path works across the affected browser or market.
- Monitoring covers the step that previously failed.
- The explanation distinguishes observed failure from inferred root cause.
