Commerce, analytics, and advertising reports answer different questions. A discrepancy is expected until period, currency, transaction identity, event collection, refunds, tax, and attribution boundaries are aligned.

## What teams usually observe

- Channel dashboards claim more revenue than the order ledger.
- Analytics misses or duplicates purchase events.
- Teams manually reconcile dashboards every week.
- Differences increase after consent, checkout, or tracking changes.

## Evidence to collect

Collect order-ledger totals and transaction identifiers, analytics events, channel reports, reporting period, timezone, currency, tax and refund treatment, consent coverage, and attribution windows.

## What not to conclude yet

<Callout type="boundary" title="Booked versus attributed">
The commerce ledger represents booked orders. Analytics represents observed events. Advertising platforms represent attributed outcomes under their own models. Do not relabel one as another.
</Callout>

## Investigation workflow

1. Define the exact period, timezone, currency, and order states.
2. Reconcile transaction identifiers before comparing totals.
3. Find missing, duplicate, late, or transformed events.
4. Document each platform's attribution window and identity model.
5. Separate collection defects from expected attribution differences.
6. Assign remediation to commerce, analytics, consent, or channel owners.

## Verification checklist

- Transaction-level matches are reproducible.
- Duplicates and missing events have evidence.
- Modeled conversions remain labeled as modeled.
- Reconciliation rules are documented for future reporting.
