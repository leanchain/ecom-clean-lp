A feed mismatch is a field-level contradiction, not a single store-wide state. Price, availability, identifiers, images, titles, shipping, and return information may each come from different transformations.

## What teams usually observe

- Channel disapprovals despite correct-looking platform data.
- Different prices or availability across the catalog, page, and feed.
- AI answers or search surfaces repeat stale product facts.
- Only particular variants or markets are affected.

## Evidence to collect

Collect the current catalog record, rendered page, structured data, feed export or channel diagnostic, variant identifiers, market, currency, and observation time.

## What not to conclude yet

Do not choose a correct value merely because one surface looks more recent. Name the authoritative system for the field or escalate the conflict to its owner.

## Investigation workflow

1. Build a field-level comparison by product and variant.
2. Trace each value to its source and transformation.
3. Check stale syncs, theme rendering, localization, and market rules.
4. Correct the authoritative object or transformation.
5. Re-export or wait for the documented channel refresh.
6. Verify the controlled page and channel diagnostic separately.

<Callout type="operator" title="System-of-record check">
When two systems disagree and ownership is unclear, report the conflict. Do not let the agent invent catalog truth.
</Callout>

## Verification checklist

- Product and variant identifiers match.
- Price and availability agree for the same market and time.
- Visible content and structured data agree.
- The channel has reprocessed the corrected data.
