A theme deployment can remove, duplicate, or contradict product markup while the visible page still looks correct.

## What teams usually observe

- Rich-result or merchant diagnostics change after deployment.
- Visible product facts remain correct while JSON-LD changes.
- Variant, price, or availability markup no longer matches the page.
- Multiple apps or theme extensions emit competing Product entities.

## Evidence to collect

Capture rendered HTML from the affected release, expected product and variant facts, parsed structured-data output, deployment changes, app and theme-extension changes, and the relevant search diagnostic.

## What not to conclude yet

Valid syntax does not guarantee eligibility, display, or correctness. A parser passing does not prove that visible content and markup describe the same offer.

## Investigation workflow

1. Parse every Product and Offer entity on the rendered page.
2. Compare visible facts with markup field by field.
3. Identify duplicates, missing relationships, and wrong variants.
4. Trace each entity to its theme or app source.
5. Correct the owning template or extension.
6. Retest the rendered page and then the external diagnostic.

## Verification checklist

- One coherent product graph describes the visible product.
- Offer facts match the active variant and market.
- Reviews and ratings are supported by visible content.
- Search-engine eligibility remains described as externally controlled.
