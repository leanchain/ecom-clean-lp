# Beseam Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-07-24

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Beseam finds the revenue leaks between an ecommerce brand's storefront, catalog, customer journeys, discovery channels, and advertising systems, then shows the team which issue to fix first.

**What it does:** Beseam connects to the commerce stack a brand already runs. It checks the customer-facing experience and the underlying commercial evidence, attaches issues to the exact product, page, query, journey, or channel involved, recommends the next action, and verifies the result after the team acts. Each verified result informs how Beseam ranks later issues.

**Product category:** Ecommerce revenue intelligence and operations
**Product type:** B2B SaaS for ecommerce brands, operators, and agencies
**Business model:** Foundation is included with every active contract. Additional products are enabled independently per tenant and store. Some customer-initiated generation uses credits; advertising media spend remains with Google or Meta.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Ecommerce brands and multi-store operators with an established commerce platform, catalog, analytics, acquisition channels, and enough operational complexity for issues to fall between tools.

**Decision-makers:** Owner/GM, ecommerce lead, growth lead, operations lead, technical operator, and agency lead.

**Primary use case:** Find the commercially important issue that no single platform can see, assign the right response, and verify what changed.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Show the owner where revenue is being lost or created and what needs attention today.
- Show the ecommerce team which products, pages, feeds, or checkout steps are not ready.
- Show the growth team whether Google or Meta campaigns can launch safely and be measured.
- Show operators whether tracking, data freshness, and storefront reliability can be trusted.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** The storefront, commerce platform, analytics, customer behavior tools, search systems, AI discovery, and advertising platforms each describe part of the business. Revenue leaks often sit between those systems, so teams see symptoms without knowing which issue matters first or whether a fix worked.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Commerce platforms know orders and catalog state but not the full customer journey.
- Analytics tools show movement but rarely connect it to a governed fix.
- Monitoring tools report technical failures without commercial priority.
- Advertising platforms report channel-native attribution, not the authoritative commerce ledger.
- Task tools track accepted work but do not find or verify the underlying issue.

**What it costs them:** Wasted acquisition spend, delayed fixes, unready products, inconsistent brand representation, repeated incidents, and time spent reconciling reports.

**Emotional tension:** Teams know something is wrong but cannot defend which problem deserves attention first.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Ecommerce intelligence and optimization platforms that cover one part of the journey but stop before a verified action-and-impact loop.
**Secondary:** Analytics, session replay, experimentation, monitoring, feed management, campaign tools, and AI visibility tools used separately.
**Indirect:** Spreadsheets, agency audits, weekly reporting, and manual cross-tool investigation.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Observes the customer-facing commerce journey across systems rather than inside one application.
- Connects evidence to the exact product, page, query, journey, campaign, or store.
- Ranks issues by commercial evidence while keeping booked, observed, attributed, and modeled money separate.
- Carries a finding through recommendation, accepted action, execution, verification, and impact.
- Keeps human approval around advertising spend and material customer-facing changes.
- Resolves product access per contract, tenant, and store.

**How we do it differently:** Beseam keeps the source evidence, commercial meaning, owner, approval, execution record, verification, and outcome attached to one issue.

**Why that's better:** The team can act without rebuilding the investigation every week and can see whether the change addressed the original problem.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| We already have analytics. | Beseam uses analytics as evidence, then connects that evidence to the exact issue, action, and verification. |
| We cannot replace our commerce stack. | Beseam connects to the stack already in place. Source systems remain authoritative for their own records. |
| We do not want an agent changing the store without approval. | Spend and material customer-facing changes retain explicit roles, approvals, preflight checks, and audit history. |
| Modeled revenue is unreliable. | Beseam labels booked, observed, attributed, and modeled money separately and discloses freshness, method, and confidence. |
| We only need some of the products. | Foundation is shared; other products are enabled independently per store and contract. |

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Repeated cross-tool investigations, conflicting reports, unowned issues, and fixes that are never rechecked.
**Pull:** One ranked issue, evidence attached to the affected object, a clear next action, and verification after the change.
**Habit:** Existing dashboards and weekly reporting already feel familiar.
**Anxiety:** Fear of replatforming, opaque AI decisions, unsafe automation, and inflated revenue claims.

## Customer Language
*The phrases below are draft problem language inferred from the product blueprint. Replace them with verbatim interview or sales-call language when available.*

**How they may describe the problem:**
- "We have the data, but we still do not know what to fix first."
- "Every platform tells a different story."
- "We fixed it, but we cannot prove whether it changed anything."
- "We find campaign blockers after the budget is approved."

**Words to use:** revenue leak, issue, evidence, product, page, store, campaign, customer journey, next action, approval, verify, booked revenue, attributed revenue, modeled opportunity, freshness.

**Words to avoid:** revenue surface, operating layer, commercial system, governed intelligence, seamless, optimize outcomes, unlock, transform, powerful, next-generation, single source of truth, autonomous growth, AI-powered.

**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Finding | Something Beseam detected |
| Recommendation | A proposed response that has not been accepted |
| Action | Accepted work with an owner and lifecycle |
| Verification | Evidence that the execution addressed the finding |
| Impact | The measured result after verification |
| Booked revenue | Revenue from authoritative commerce orders |
| Observed revenue | Revenue-related evidence observed outside the commerce ledger |
| Attributed revenue | Revenue assigned by a disclosed attribution method |
| Modeled opportunity | An estimate with disclosed method and confidence |
