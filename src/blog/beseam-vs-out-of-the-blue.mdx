*Last updated: Feb 20, 2026. This comparison is based on publicly available product pages and Shopify app listings, plus the research you shared.*

## TL;DR

Out Of The Blue is positioned as **real-time monitoring + alerting** (especially around pixels/UTMs/checkout signals).

Beseam is positioned as **monitoring + attribution to Shopify changes + guided rollback**—so agencies can ship faster without turning every deploy into a firefight.

## What Out Of The Blue claims

- Real-time monitoring with alerts (Slack/SMS/email).
- Strong framing around pixels/UTMs/checkout failures.
- “Deep RCA” style messaging, but change-linked attribution to Shopify diffs is not the central promise in its Shopify listing.

Out Of The Blue also publishes self-serve pricing on its Shopify listing (Free and paid tiers), which matters for agencies selling monitoring as a line item across multiple stores.

## The key difference: alerts vs incident response

### Out Of The Blue (monitoring-first)

- Great at saying “something broke” (and often where).
- Best when your main pain is **visibility** and fast notification.

### Beseam (control-plane-first)

Beseam is designed to answer “what now?”:

- tie the incident to an exact **store-controlled change** (theme publish, promo eligibility, pixel/script edits, etc.)
- produce a **runbook** for the most likely fixes
- enable safe rollback workflows and staged rollouts

## For SEO/GEO agencies

Beseam is built for the changes SEO/GEO agencies ship every week:

- schema & FAQ updates
- PDP copy/module changes
- feed/policy-driven changes that impact discovery

And it keeps those changes **guardrailed on revenue**.
