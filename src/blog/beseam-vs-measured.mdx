## TL;DR

Measured is an enterprise platform for **testing + modeling + optimization** (a measurement cycle: measure → test → optimize).

Beseam is a Shopify-first **incident response layer for store changes**: detect conversion regressions, link them to a specific change diff, and guide rollback/runbooks.

## What Measured claims (from the research you shared)

- Full-stack platform combining testing, MMM, optimization, benchmarking.
- Incrementality testing (geo-matched + split tests).
- Cross-channel “single source of truth” dashboards and planning/optimization.

## Why Beseam is complementary (not a replacement)

Even the best measurement stack doesn’t stop revenue loss caused by:

- a theme publish removing a PDP module
- a discount rule changing eligibility
- a checkout extension misbehaving
- a pixel/script change breaking tracking or checkout UX

Beseam treats those as first-class “interventions” with:

- a normalized change log
- impact monitoring
- change-linked attribution
- rollback/runbooks + recovery verification
