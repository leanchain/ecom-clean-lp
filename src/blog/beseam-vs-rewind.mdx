## TL;DR

Rewind is a default choice when you need **reliable backup + restore** and want a suite that also includes monitoring/QA/staging concepts.

Beseam is built to answer a different question: when conversion drops after a change, **which change caused it** and **what should we roll back**—with a runbook.

## What Rewind claims (based on the research you provided)

- Backup/restore for key Shopify data (and one-click restore language).
- “Protection Suite” style features including monitoring, automated QA, and a staging environment.
- Bulk product change alerts and GA4 monitoring are part of the broader value story in your notes.

## Why agencies still need change-to-incident intelligence

Backups are necessary—but they don’t solve day-to-day incident response:

- You still need to diagnose “what changed” (diff)
- You still need to pick the correct rollback target quickly
- You still need a repeatable runbook across many clients and teams

That’s the operating layer Beseam is aiming to own.

## Where Beseam complements (or outflanks)

- Beseam can integrate with backup tools as the “restore safety net.”
- Beseam focuses on Shopify change diffs + attribution + incident workflows so teams can revert the *specific bad change* fast.
