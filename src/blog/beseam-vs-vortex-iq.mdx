*Last updated: Feb 20, 2026. This comparison is based on publicly available product pages and Shopify app listings, plus the research you shared.*

## TL;DR

If you want the **broadest “full loop” story** (detect → diagnose → execute with rollback) across many signals, Vortex IQ is positioned as one of the strongest end-to-end options.

Beseam’s wedge is narrower and more operational: a **Shopify-first change-to-incident control plane** focused on *PDP upgrades + guardrails*—with incident attribution to store-controlled diffs and guided rollback/runbooks.

## What Vortex IQ claims

- **Real-time anomaly detection** (low-latency).
- **Root-cause correlation** that can reference “what changed” (including script changes / checkout failures as examples).
- **Staging + backup + rollback** as part of controlled execution.

Vortex IQ also publishes pricing tiers on its site (e.g. Free, $49/month, $149/month) and ships Shopify apps for backup and staging. Those are useful anchors for agencies evaluating “how real is the execution layer?”.

## Where Vortex IQ looks strong

- Broad “connect signals across tools” platform narrative.
- Execution layer language (staging/backup/rollback) is already central to the product story.
- Credible if your organization wants a **multi-system AI ops platform**, not only Shopify.

## Where Beseam is different (and why agencies care)

### 1) Shopify-native change graph (the “diff” is the product)

Beseam is built around a **normalized Shopify change graph** for the objects that tend to break conversion outcomes:

- Theme publishes & theme asset diffs
- App embeds/blocks and critical PDP modules
- Pixels/script changes
- Discounts/promos and eligibility
- Shipping/delivery customizations
- (Over time) Functions versions and other high-risk objects

### 2) Incident → change attribution with evidence

Beseam is designed to answer, for every incident:

1. **What changed?** (exact diff)
2. **Who/what made it?** (agency, internal, app, automation)
3. **What did it do to KPIs?** (rev/session, checkout start, conversion)
4. **What should we do now?** (keep / rollback / staged rollout / iterate)

### 3) Runbooks + reversible deploys for agency workflows

Instead of “alerts and a dashboard,” Beseam is built to support agency reality:

- stage 1 PDP → monitor 24–48h → scale to 10 → scale to catalog
- “this change is risky” → auto-guardrails → rollback readiness
- reproducible runbooks so your team isn’t reinventing incident response per client

## When to choose which

- Choose **Vortex IQ** if you want a broad “AI OS” that spans many systems and an execution story across them.
- Choose **Beseam** if you want Shopify-first **PDP upgrade + protect** workflows, with conversion-ops guardrails and change-linked incident response.
