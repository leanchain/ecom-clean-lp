## TL;DR

Rockerbox is positioned as a “platform of record” for **marketing measurement**, with a data foundation and toolkits like MMM/MTA/testing.

Beseam is positioned as a **Shopify change-to-incident control plane**: detect KPI regressions caused by store-controlled changes, attribute to diffs, and guide rollback/runbooks.

## What Rockerbox claims (from the research you shared)

- Unified toolkit: MTA + MMM + testing.
- Data foundation: centralized taxonomy + integrations.
- Shopify integration exists (orders + web traffic), and pixel compatibility via checkout extensibility.
- MMM can incorporate promotions as variables (modeled inputs), not as managed interventions.

## The difference: modeling a promo vs capturing the promo change event

If you’re an agency, you typically want both:

- “Did the promo help?” (measurement)
- “Did the promo change break eligibility / hurt checkout?” (incident response)

Beseam focuses on the second problem by ingesting the **actual change event** and tying it to conversion anomalies with evidence and rollback paths.
