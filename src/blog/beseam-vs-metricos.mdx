## TL;DR

Metricos is positioned as **change logging + performance context** (“connect the dots” between changes and outcomes).

Beseam is positioned as a **change-to-incident control plane**: detect KPI regressions, attribute to a specific Shopify diff with confidence, and guide rollback/remediation.

## What Metricos claims (based on the research you provided)

- Tracks changes like theme edits, product updates, and price adjustments (per your notes).
- Framing around connecting changes to performance.
- Less explicit about real-time incident detection and rollback as first-class workflows.

## The key difference for agencies

### Metricos (journal + context)

- Useful for “what happened recently” and putting notes next to changes.
- Great for visibility and change history.

### Beseam (operational guardrails)

- versions changes and ships them safely (staged rollout)
- watches KPIs post-deploy (rev/session, checkout-start, conversion)
- triggers incidents with likely-cause ranking tied to diffs
- provides runbooks + rollback guidance when revenue dips
