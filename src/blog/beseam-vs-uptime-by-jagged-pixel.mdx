*Last updated: Feb 20, 2026. This comparison is based on publicly available product pages and Shopify app listings, plus the research you shared.*

## TL;DR

Uptime is a strong option when your #1 pain is **preventing breakages** via automated UI tests, especially around theme updates.

Beseam’s focus is a step downstream: when performance drops or conversion regresses, Beseam is built to **link the regression to the exact Shopify change diff** and guide rollback/remediation.

## What Uptime claims (based on the research you provided)

- Synthetic monitoring + downtime monitoring.
- Automated UI tests, including the ability to run tests on theme update.
- Slack alerts and monitoring around critical journeys (ATC/checkout).

## The operational gap: “it broke” vs “what changed and what do we do now?”

### Uptime (prevention-first)

- Great: catch obvious functional breaks quickly.
- Best: tight feedback loop for engineering teams shipping UI changes.

### Beseam (change intelligence + incident response)

- versions and diffs every risky change (not only theme updates)
- watches revenue KPIs after deploys
- attributes regressions to recent store changes with evidence
- provides rollback/runbooks that agencies can execute reliably
